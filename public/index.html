The things people ask

Before you
book.

Still unsure? Send a note to hello@preciousmoments.ph and a real person will get back to you.

How do I reserve a date?

Send a booking request with your date and pieces. We confirm availability and send the reservation details. A 50% reservation fee locks the date; the balance is settled before or on the event day.

How long does printing take?

Small prints, IDs, and photocopies are usually same-day. Tarps, shirts, signboards, and invitations need 2–5 working days depending on quantity and material. Rush work is quoted separately.

Do you cover events outside the studio?

Yes. Photo, video, and booth crews travel. Out-of-town bookings include a travel fee that we confirm before you reserve.

How do payments work?

We accept cash, GCash, and bank transfer. Your dashboard shows exactly what has been recorded as paid, so there is no guessing. Official receipts are issued for every payment.

Can I move my booking?

Message the studio as early as you can. One move to another open date is free when made at least 7 days before the event; material costs already incurred may be charged.
